# machete: where we are and the plan (2026-10-02)

## Where we are

| version | change | measured | lichess |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.3.3 | repertoire | no cost | 36.8% (102 games) |
| 0.3.4 | network C30 (+40M farm positions) | +24 ± 24 | 44.4% (62 games) |
| 0.3.5 | network C33 (+10.5M more) | +5 ± 11 (4,000 games) | 45.2% (83 games) |
| 0.3.6 | king-danger search fix | +2 ± 17, fixes a mate walked into | live now |

What stopped working: **more of the same data**. C34 (all 19.3M of the Pi's run) is −3 ± 17 against C33. Self-play positions from the broad book have reached their plateau at this network size.

What the games say (80 draws and losses reviewed at depth 22):

- 36 of 80 costliest moves are **evaluation errors** (machete repeats them at depth 16);
- 22 of 80 are **search or time errors** (at depth 16 it finds Stockfish's move, but not in the game);
- the draws are level games, not thrown wins: 31 of 32 end level, only 3 ever had machete winning;
- as White machete leaves the opening +0.25 and drifts to −0.16 by ply 72; as Black it scores 8–9–19 on 0.3.5;
- it is 15–20 points of clock behind from move 10 (6.6 s a move against booked opponents' 2.9 s).

## The plan: four tracks, one change per version

### 1. Openings: narrow and deep (your strategy), starting with the Kalashnikov

Over 3.17 billion equal-strength games (LAION), Black scores 44.2% on average. The **Kalashnikov Sicilian** (1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 e5) scores **51.5%** over 47M games, the only Black system where Black outscores White. Its sister the Sveshnikov scores 47.9% and tied for first in our own test against full Stockfish (11.0%). The Najdorf, Taimanov, Dragon and Kan are all at 42–43%; the Marshall's parent the Ruy Lopez is 45.1%; the Grünfeld 43.0%.

- **Now:** the Kalashnikov's own test against full Stockfish (queued), and a generation book of its positions and the anti-Sicilians White can choose after 2...Nc6.
- **0.3.7 candidate:** the repertoire's `black marshall` lines replaced by Kalashnikov lines, taken from the most-played and best-scoring LAION continuations. Measured on lichess (Black's score against 1.e4), because self-play cannot measure a repertoire.
- **Then:** the same LAION ranking for Black against 1.d4 (the Grünfeld is below average, and four of four Grünfelds against weaker bots were drawn) and for White.

### 2. Data: a different kind, not more of the same

- **pi-02 and the PC** generate Stockfish 19 games from the Kalashnikov and 2...Nc6 anti-Sicilian positions: the territory machete will actually play in. pi-01 finishes its broad run (the last of that kind).
- **A repertoire network** (C35): C33's recipe plus that data. Tested by SPRT like every network, and on the Kalashnikov test against full Stockfish, which is where it should show.
- **First, the noise floor:** C33 trained again with another seed (running on the GPU). C33's +18 and C34's −3 on nearly the same data say one training run's luck is large; every network decision after this needs that number.

### 3. Search: the 22 of 80

- **0.3.6** is the first of these: the king-danger fix.
- **Exploit mode** (attack plan step 2): when the opponent's last move raised our score by 80 cp or more, search quiet attacking moves deeper. Yardstick: the 3,114-position attack suite, where machete finds 74.5% overall but only 68% of quiet moves and 63% of sacrifices.
- **The clock:** the opening discount measured nothing in self-play (both sides are out of book there). It needs a test against a booked opponent before it can be kept or dropped: a match where the opponent plays its first ten moves instantly from a book.

### 4. Speed: the thing that unlocks a bigger network

A wider network lost at 282M positions because the evaluation got slower. Mach 6.7.2 adds 256-bit vectors and GPU compute: a faster evaluation in the engine, and the Mach trainer on the GPU. **It needs your OK to download** (mach-6.7.2-x86_64-windows.zip, 2.9 MB).

## Kept honest

- Every release is one change and names its test.
- Openings are compared against the same opponent or over equal-strength games, never by raw lichess score.
- A network is accepted on an SPRT against the previous one **and** a packaged head-to-head; C33 showed why one test is not enough.
